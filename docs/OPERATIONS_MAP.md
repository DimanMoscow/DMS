## Performance baseline before optimization

The values below preserve the historical v54 scheduled-health record. They are total handler times, not a percentile or a component trace.

| Path | Observed production total | Work visible from source | What is and is not proven |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| Morning digest | 12.854 s at 08:10:54 | inline Calendar sync, dashboard, attention summary, Telegram send | total is proven; lock/Sheets/Calendar/Telegram shares are unknown |
| Evening queue | 22.109 s at 22:52:23 | inline Calendar sync, queue view, Telegram send | total is proven; the reason it exceeded morning is not measured |
| Hourly Calendar sync | 7.852 s at `2026-09-09T20:21:19.759Z` | pre-reconciliation, plan, per-row writes, flush, post-reconciliation | total is proven; component and volume data are absent |
| Automatic backup | completed in its expected window | reads/copies/validates protected sheets and records scheduled success | exact duration was not preserved in checkpoint docs |
| Native financial write/recalculate/read verification | 2.0–3.1 s; tenfold fixture 2.26 s | isolated Sheets write, recalculation and read-back | includes API latency; it is not a production request latency sample |
| MiniApp API | PII-free per-request total exists in Vercel logs | Vercel validation/proxy plus Apps Script action | no aggregated v54 baseline or upstream component split is checked into Git |

Static work amplification explains the first optimization target without pretending to explain each live duration:

- Production v54 performs Calendar reconciliation before planning, a second Calendar listing for the plan, and reconciliation after the write. Each path scans from the persistent start date in `Настройки!B15` through tomorrow, so the window grows over the lifetime of the system. This is at least three full-history Calendar listings per successful sync. Released v56 changes the plan listing to `updatedMin` with a 24-hour overlap, bounds ordinary verification to 120 days, and runs that wider listing at most daily; a 410 cursor rejection alone falls back to the original full range.
- The same sync reads the operational sheets needed by reconciliation and planning, including full Queue and Journal ranges, more than once.
- `applyCalendarQueueSyncPlan_()` issues one `setValues()` call per changed queue row and may copy a template per inserted row.
- There are 21 static `syncCalendarToQueue()` call sites in the audited Telegram sources, including the morning/evening handlers. This creates avoidable lock contention and repeated ingestion around the hourly owner.
- v54 records only total scheduled duration. It does not measure lock wait, Sheets reads/writes, Calendar calls, Telegram calls, reconciliation time, row/event counts, or retries. v56 now records bounded, PII-free component metrics; comparable production percentile samples remain unavailable.

The largest measured totals are evening queue (22.109 s), morning digest (12.854 s), Calendar sync (7.852 s), then the isolated native financial interval (2.0–3.1 s). Calendar work amplification is a proven cause of unnecessary service calls. Its exact contribution to the three scheduled totals remains unmeasured.

## Released recovery and remaining boundaries

- v56 shares immutable queue/day commands and results between Telegram and MiniApp. Per-row semantic preconditions preserve accepted unchanged rows across technical metadata changes and new Queue rows. A changed row is excluded safely; accepted payload and identity binding never change.
- Random render-bound `qv` row/day buttons accept their first click into the existing cf2 ledger. Nonce/TTL, admin/chat/message/action binding, ScriptLock, durable result, replay protection and fail-closed semantics remain. Presentation happens after durable commit and lock release.
- Calendar ingestion uses a bounded overlap cursor, cancellation/move handling and conditional writes. Narrow owner-bound scheduled start/failure telemetry records maintenance errors without reopening business writes. Scheduled freshness requires natural executions.
- New conducted singles settle through `planDmsSingleSettlement_` / `writeDmsSingleSettlement_` once; existing full credit is honored and partial/ambiguous effects require review. Block creation writes validated total and unit price together.
- Owner recovery preview/apply is a control-plane exception: exact private accepted plan, current identity/entitlement and Calendar guards, fresh backup/restore, paused/drained shared lock, formula protection, durable result and one audit record. It is absent from Telegram/MiniApp routes.
- Semantic financial reconciliation, binding reactivation and measurement corruption isolation are deployed. See `docs/ACCEPTANCE_MATRIX.md` and `docs/EMERGENCY_RECOVERY.md` for evidence.
- Residual boundaries D4–D6 remain explicit: independent onboarding/management creation, inline ingestion on some Telegram reads, and manual spreadsheet/repair paths. They were not silently rewritten or claimed fully unified. Do not use `/today`, `/attention`, or calendar-refresh callbacks to manufacture natural scheduled evidence.

## Performance after recovery

The production-sized and 10x isolated fixtures confirm ten unchanged accepted rows with 27 storage reads in both cases. Semantic-precondition reads fell from 5,404 to 2,425 cells at 1x and from 52,060 to 24,250 cells at 10x. Rendering and network delivery occur after the shared mutation lock is released. These are service-boundary measurements, not a production latency percentile.

The first v56 read-only live gate took 11.968 s. Real Telegram Yesterday and authorized MiniApp Today/client navigation were exercised without business writes. Natural v56 Calendar sync completed in 9.142 s (generation work 8.426 s). One natural watchdog took 283.714 s, including 279.238 s in health checks and no mutation lock held; the subsequent owner live gate took 17.178 s. This outlier has no established cause and remains an operational latency risk. Historical morning/evening totals must not be presented as comparable v56 benchmarks.
